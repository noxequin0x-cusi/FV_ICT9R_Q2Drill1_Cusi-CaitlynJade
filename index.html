Beginner's Guide to Badminton



Essential Equipment

  • Badminton racket
  • Shuttlecock

Basic Rules & Scoring

  • Format: Played as singles
  • Scoring: Best of 3 games to 21 points
  • Serving: Even scores serve from the right, odd scores serve from the left
